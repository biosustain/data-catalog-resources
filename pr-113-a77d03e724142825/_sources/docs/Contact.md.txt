# Contact

If you have questions about **Data Catalog**, encounter issues, or have suggestions for improvements and new features, you can contact us [here](mailto:dinghe@dtu.dk?cc=pasdom@dtu.dk&subject=Data%20Catalog%20-%20Question%2FIssue%2FSuggestion&body=Type%3A%20%28Question%20%2F%20Issue%20%2F%20Suggestion%29%0D%0A%0D%0ADescription%3A%0D%0A%0D%0ASteps%20to%20reproduce%20%28if%20applicable%29%3A%0D%0A%0D%0AScreenshots%2Ferror%20messages%20%28if%20applicable%29%).

Include:
* A brief description of your question, issue, or suggestion
* Any relevant details or context
* Steps to reproduce the issue (if applicable)
* Screenshots or error messages (if possible)

We are always open to feedback and ideas!
